Chord–Scale Tree

Pick a note, a chord on it, and a scale that has that chord as its home. Then pick any chord inside the scale to branch again. Every chord can become a new root.

Scales come from the 21 modes of the major, harmonic minor and melodic minor scales. A chord is listed under a scale when stacking thirds from that scale's first note produces it. Dashed chords are not the home chord of any of these modes.
